The Dimensions of Sexuality

Sexuality is a core part of being human. It is multifaceted, diverse, and goes far beyond just who we are attracted to. True understanding comes from exploring its many distinct layers.

❤️ Biological Sex

The physical and physiological attributes we are born with. This includes our chromosomes, hormone levels, and anatomical characteristics. It is typically assigned at birth by medical professionals.

🔮 Gender Identity

A person's deeply felt, internal sense of being male, female, a blend of both, or neither. This is how you perceive yourself and what you call yourself, which may or may not align with your assigned sex at birth.

🎨 Gender Expression

How a person presents their gender to the world. This includes external behaviors, clothing choices, hairstyles, voice, and body language. It is a creative space for personal presentation.

✨ Sexual Orientation

An inherent emotional, romantic, or sexual attraction to other people. This includes identities like heterosexual, gay, lesbian, bisexual, asexual, and pansexual, describing the direction of our attractions.
